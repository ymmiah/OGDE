THE PLATFORM

One ecosystem. Six connected capabilities.

Built as interoperable components, starting with a small, verifiable, read-only security loop.

01 / INTELLIGENCE
✧

Verified threat intelligence

Authenticated advisory sources, evidence provenance, freshness checks and publisher trust controls. A valid signature identifies a publisher; it does not prove a claim is true.

02 / DISCOVERY
⌘

Local asset discovery

Owner-authorised WordPress, Linux and software dependency inventory. Sensitive inventory remains local unless an operator explicitly chooses otherwise.

03 / PRIORITY
◈

Context-aware risk engine

Evidence-led findings that consider affected versions, exploit signals, exposure, criticality, uncertainty and VEX statements.

04 / ASSISTANCE
✳

AI security analyst

Planned optional assistance for explaining findings and proposing mitigations, grounded in source evidence and clearly separated from trusted policy decisions.

05 / GOVERNANCE
▣

Human-approved response

Auditable review queues, recommendations and eventually narrowly scoped response integrations that require explicit authorisation.

06 / COLLABORATION
◎

Federated defence network

A future network for verifiable intelligence exchange between independent organisations, with privacy, revocation and publisher governance.

HOW IT WORKS

Evidence in. Defensible decisions out.

The architecture is designed to keep customer systems under customer control.

STEP 01

Receive

Load independently authenticated intelligence and check its provenance, freshness and integrity.

STEP 02

Match locally

Compare evidence with an authorised software inventory without automatically uploading assets.

STEP 03

Explain risk

Produce traceable findings, confidence levels and actionable review context.

STEP 04

Review & decide

Keep consequential decisions with authorised people and retain audit evidence.

REAL-WORLD USE CASE

A vulnerable WordPress plugin appears in a security advisory.

OGDE aims to verify the advisory, compare affected versions against a local site inventory, consider exploitation evidence and produce a reviewable priority. It should not flag every installation as compromised or silently change a website.

NON-NEGOTIABLE PRINCIPLES

Security without unnecessary control.

01 / PRIVACY

Keep inventory local

No default upload of customer asset inventories.

02 / PROVENANCE

Verify independently

Publisher identity and evidence authenticity require independently established trust.

03 / GOVERNANCE

Human decisions

No unapproved infrastructure modification or automatic escalation.

04 / ACCOUNTABILITY

Explain every finding

Traceable evidence, confidence and explicit limitations.
